Delete your AiO Bible account

You can request permanent deletion of your AiO Bible account and the data associated with it by following the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email aiobible11@gmail.com from the email address connected to your AiO Bible account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my AiO Bible account.
  3. Include the email address connected to your account. Do not send your password, sign-in code, or other sensitive credentials.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before we complete the deletion.
Request account deletion

Data that will be deleted

After verification, we will delete data associated with your account, including:

  • Your account profile, including your name, email address, profile image reference, and account identifier
  • Saved favorites and account-linked preferences
  • Quiz progress, scores, and account-linked activity history
  • Comments, requests, and other content associated with your account
  • Your notification token and other account-linked application identifiers

Processing time and retention

We normally complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some information may remain temporarily in protected backups and will be removed through our normal backup cycle within 90 days.

We may retain limited information when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Any retained data will be restricted to those purposes and kept only as long as necessary.

Information stored on your device

Account deletion does not remove information stored only on your device. You can clear AiO Bible's app data in your device settings or uninstall the app to remove locally stored information.

Contact us

For questions about account or data deletion, email aiobible11@gmail.com.
